Final-year research project · Uganda Christian University

Sorting waste where it is thrown away

SortBin is a low-cost smart bin for offices and schools. A camera and a lightweight neural network recognise each item as organic, plastic, metal or general, a rotating tray routes it to the matching chamber, and sensors report when each chamber needs emptying.
