Sort cycle

  1. Flap opens and the waste lands on the tray
  2. Light turns on and the camera takes a picture
  3. Microprocessor classifies the waste
  4. Motor turns the tray 45° clockwise or anticlockwise
  5. Servos tip one long side of the tray down
  6. Waste slides off into the chamber
  7. Tray levels and the motor returns home
