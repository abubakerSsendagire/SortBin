Datasets

RealWaste (Single, Iranmanesh & Raad, 2023) · The Garbage Dataset (Kunwar, Kaggle release) · TrashNet (Yang & Thung, 2016). Used in line with their licences.

Tools

TensorFlow & Keras, MobileNetV2 (ImageNet weights), Google Colab, DroidCam for the prototype camera stream, Three.js for the 3D simulation.

Acknowledgements

The lecturers and the Department of Computing & Technology at Uganda Christian University, and classmates for their feedback and support.

Ethics

The camera faces only the tray; images with people or personal information are deleted. Hazardous items are out of scope.
